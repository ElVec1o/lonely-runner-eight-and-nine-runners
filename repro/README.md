# Reproduction directory: the eight- and nine-runner theorems in Lean 4

All paths are relative to this directory (`repro/`). The Lean project is `../lean` (override with `LEAN_DIR`); the eight-runner certificates are in `eight/` (see `eight/README.md`).
Labels: **observed** = run on 19 November 2026 on the development machine (macOS, 24 GB, 10 cores) and the output read; **recorded** = a figure or printout from the development records, not re-run for this directory; **not done** = not run.

## 1. What is claimed
`LRSpectrumFull.NineSound.Plan9.Assemble9Final.nine_runners_unconditional` (module `LRSpectrumFull/NineSound/Assemble9Final.lean`):
`forall v : Fin 8 -> Int, (forall i, 0 < v i) -> 1/9 <= ML v`, **no hypothesis**, axioms `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.
Its proof is `Assemble9.nine_runners_final : LRC7 -> ...` applied to `Assemble9Final.lrc7_of_eight`, which is `EightRunnersReal.eight_runners_unconditional_real` (`forall v : Fin 7 -> Int, (forall i, 0 < v i) -> 1/8 <= ML v`) with `1/((6:Real)+2) = 1/8`. `LRC7 := forall u : Fin 7 -> Int, (forall i, 0 < u i) -> 1/((6:Real)+2) <= ML u`.
`PrimeData p` for the 36 primes of `S36` is proved by the 36 generated modules `NineSound/G<p>/PD.lean`, assembled by `Assemble9.pd36`. The eight-runner theorem uses `BarajasSerra.LRC6_barajasSerra` (seven runners) and the 27 kernel-checked statements `lemma7Check 7 p = true` of `eight/`.
`ML` is defined in `MaxLoneliness.lean`, `NearestInteger.lean`; `check_axioms.lean` prints the definitions, `LRC7` and `PrimeData` with `#print`. Read them before trusting the English.
Not claimed: any new case of the lonely runner conjecture (both cases are known from computer-assisted proofs in the literature), other numbers of runners, peer review.

## 6. Regeneration (observed)
`regen_data.sh` compiles `generators/gen_prime12.rs` and `gen_prime13.rs` (`rustc -O`), runs the generator named in `PARAMS.tsv` for each prime (1 to 7 s and below 100 MB per prime), compares every produced `.lean` file with the sha256 in `MANIFEST.tsv`, and regenerates `Assemble9.lean` with `generators/mkfinal.sh`. Results in `expected_output/regen_summary.txt`: all 36 primes regenerated (91 s of generator time in total on the loaded development machine, below 100 MB per run); **25,852 files identical to the manifest (25,851 per-prime modules plus `Assemble9.lean`), 0 different**; the 36 control modules `Ctl9.lean` that the generators also emit are outside the import closure and not in the manifest. With `--install`, the 7,849 omitted modules were written into a scratch copy of the shipped layout and `build.sh --plan` then printed `PLAN VALID` (`expected_output/plan_after_regen.txt`); in the shipped state it prints `PLAN INCOMPLETE`, exit 4 (`expected_output/plan_shipped_state.txt`). These figures were measured for the 25,960-module closure before the eight-runner modules were added; the 25,852 files and the plan checks are unchanged by the addition (the added modules are shipped and hand-written), and the plan was re-run on the final layout (`expected_output/plan_shipped_state.txt`).
Eight runners: `eight/stage/regen_e8stage.sh` regenerates the staged sources of the 27 primes and compares every file with `eight/E8STAGE.tsv` (see `expected_output/regen_e8stage.txt` and `eight/README.md`).
Separately (a run in the development tree, not a shipped script): the cleaned nine-runner generators were run for all 36 primes with the parameters of `PARAMS.tsv` and every produced file was compared with the corresponding file of the development tree from which the libraries were built, after replacing the old header text of line 1 by the new one: **25,923 files compared, 0 different**. The generator sources of earlier generations (`gen_prime9`, `gen_prime10`, `gen_tab`) are not shipped: no module of the final trees comes from them.

## 7. How the final assembly was elaborated, and what it cost (observed once, 19 November 2026)
`assemble_final.sh` documents the three raw-lean steps (`--plan` checks the inputs and prints the commands; `--run` does them), `assemble.sh` the nine-runner step alone. Each step is one `lean` process with a `LEAN_PATH` whose first entry is a farm of symbolic links over the lake library directory, with `NineSound/G<p>` taken from the restored per-prime archives (lake reports the restored modules as out of date, because the archives contain no `ir/*.c` files) and the staged eight-runner libraries as the last entry. The `.olean` of each step is written into the farm and used by the next.

| step | module | wall | peak RSS |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `EightRunnersReal` (imports 27 staged libraries, `EightE5`, `BarajasSerraLRC6Plan`) | 127 s | 4.53 GB |
| 2 | `NineSound.Assemble9` (36 primes) | 91 s | 5.57 GB |
| 3 | `NineSound.Assemble9Final` | 112 s | 5.91 GB |
| controls | `LRC7 := sorry`; bridge with 1/7; positivity dropped; seven-speed theorem on eight speeds | 134 s, 114 s | 5.29, 6.14 GB |

Earlier measurements of the nine-runner step alone (recorded): 36 primes 76 s, 5.76 GB; import of `Final9` only 14.0 s, 5.50 GB; 2 primes 28.5 s; 4 primes 5.8 s; control with `primeData_241 := sorry` 69 s, 6.29 GB.
**Limits.** The inputs of the three steps, the 36 archives (`ARCHIVES.sha256`, 641 MB) and the 949 staged oleans (`eight/E8STAGE_OLEANS.sha256`, 2.5 GB), are not shipped and were **not recompiled in the final run**; the final oleans of the three steps were deleted after the run. `assemble_final.sh --plan` against the development tree printed `ASSEMBLE-FINAL PLAN VALID` (36 of 36 archives match, 27 of 27 staged `Asm.olean` present); `--run` was **not** executed on the shipped files.

## 8. Library modules with axioms and `sorry` (read before scanning the sources)
The closure of `Assemble9Final` includes `EightCert27.lean`, which declares 27 axioms `lemma7Check_seven_p<p>_verified` (a compaction used by an earlier route), and superseded stubs: `EightPlan.e5_sum_STUB_SUPERSEDED` and the "Dream lemma" of `EightMSS` use `sorry` (one line each), and `BarajasSerra.lean` contains one unfinished lemma with `sorry`; the proof of `LRC6_barajasSerra` does not use it. `EightE5` imports `EightCert27`, so these declarations are in the environment, but `#print axioms` of the four headline theorems lists only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, and the planted-`sorry` controls show that a `sorry` below a theorem would be visible. `EightE5.eight_runners_unconditional` (the earlier route) does depend on the 27 axioms and is not used. `expected_output/source_scan_eight.txt` lists the code lines of these modules found by the scan.
