# repro/eight: the 27 eight-runner certificates

The eight-runner theorem `LRSpectrumFull.EightRunnersReal.eight_runners_unconditional_real` (`1/8 <= ML v` for seven positive integer speeds) rests on
(a) the seven-runner theorem `BarajasSerra.LRC6_barajasSerra` (ordinary lake-built modules in `../../lean`),
(b) the reduction `EightPlan` / `EightE5` (`EightE5.eight_runners_of`, which takes the seven-runner theorem and the 27 checks as hypotheses), and
(c) the 27 statements `lemma7Check 7 p = true`, `p` in `{31, 37, 43, 47, 53, 59, 61, 67, 71, 73, 79, 83, 89, 97, 101, 103, 107, 109, 113, 127, 131, 137, 139, 149, 151, 157, 163}`.
This directory holds everything about (c). Each statement is proved by `decide +kernel` (no `native_decide`) in generated staged modules `E8Stage.P<p>.{Base,Tab,Top,S*,Asm}`; the checker is `LRSpectrumFull/EightCheckV4.lean` and its soundness is `EightCheckSound.lean` (both in `../../lean`). For each prime, `E8Stage.P<p>.Asm` proves `LRSpectrumFull.EightV4.Cert<p>.lemma7Check_seven_p<p>`, and `#print axioms` of it prints `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` (recorded in `records/P<p>/record.txt`).
The staged modules are **not** part of the lake package and not part of `MANIFEST.tsv`: they were compiled with plain `lean` into a staging directory (`stage/compile1.sh`, `stage/run_prime.sh`), and `EightRunnersReal` imports them from there (`repro/assemble_final.sh`, `stage/assemble_real.sh`).

## What is shipped and what is not
The staged sources are large (1577 MB for the 27 primes, up to about 182 MB for p = 163), so they are not shipped. They are reproduced exactly by the generator:

```
bash stage/regen_e8stage.sh              # builds the generator (rustc), regenerates each prime in a scratch directory, compares every file with E8STAGE.tsv
bash stage/regen_e8stage.sh 31 37        # selected primes
bash stage/regen_e8stage.sh --install D  # keep the sources in D/E8Stage/P<p>/ (needs the free disk of all selected primes)
```

## What was observed, and what was not (19 November 2026)
* **Observed (Rust and shell, no Lean):** for all 27 primes, the generator binary of the development tree (built from the original `l7v4.rs`) reproduces the combined sha256 recorded in `records/P<p>/record.txt` (27 of 27); the shipped, cleaned generator `generator/l7v4.rs` produces the same files except line 1 (lines 2 to the end are identical, hashed per file), and its line 1 is the expected header (0 differences, 0 bad headers; 949 files). `E8STAGE.tsv` was written from the output of the cleaned generator.
* **Original sources versus shipped sources.** The generator used to emit a first-line comment naming a path in the development tree. The shipped generator emits `/- GENERATED by l7v4.rs p=<p> mode=stage -/`. So the sources of `E8STAGE.tsv` differ from the sources that were compiled in September 2026 in line 1 only, and the `combined_sha256` of the records (a hash of the concatenated original sources) is not the hash of the shipped sources. The `generator:`, `checker:` and `soundness:` hashes in the records are those of the development files at that time (the generator before cleaning; `EightCheckSound.lean` and others later had comments edited, see `../EDITED_COMMENTS.tsv`).
* **Not re-run:** the kernel checks. The 27 staged libraries (949 `.olean` files, 2.5 GB) were compiled on 29 and 30 September 2026 (`record.real.txt`: staged build with `KEEP=1`, sources deleted afterwards, oleans kept) and were not recompiled for this release; the final assembly links against them. `E8STAGE_OLEANS.sha256` and `records/P<p>/olean_sizes.txt` record what was linked. The kernel wall times recorded per prime (`E8STAGE_PRIMES.tsv`) are those of the September builds (two lean processes, about 1 GB each); they were not re-measured.
* `stage/*.sh` are the scripts of the development tree with paths made relative to this repository (`lean/` is the lake project); `run_prime.sh` and `compile1.sh` call `lake env` in `../../lean` to find the Lean path and use at most two lean processes machine-wide.
